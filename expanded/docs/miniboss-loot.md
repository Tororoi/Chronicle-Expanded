# Miniboss loot

What a miniboss drops in place of money ([#154](https://github.com/Tororoi/Chronicle-Expanded/pull/154)): its kind's
rare drop 5% of the time and its common drop 30%. An item marked with a dungeon drops from there on, and → gives way to
a better one in a later dungeon. Otherwise, or when none of its items drops in this dungeon yet, it drops the back
floor's key (35%), one of the dungeon's weapons, a gem or attachment, or a powder, Stamina Drink or Dran's Feather.
The tables are `expanded/src/enemies/miniboss_loot.cpp`'s.
